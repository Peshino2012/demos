FAQ

What clients ask us most

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation over WhatsApp is free. If we need a meeting to review documents, we'll tell you the fee beforehand.

I was fired. What should I do first?

Don't sign anything you don't understand, and keep pay stubs, letters and messages. Contact us and we'll tell you how to respond.

Do you take cases outside the city?

We do video consultations statewide. For cases in other states we work with partner firms.
